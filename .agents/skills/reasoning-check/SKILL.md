# /reasoning-check: Rigorous Truth-Seeking Reasoning Audit

Audits complex claims, pedagogical designs, or system modifications against ground-truth evidence before action is taken.

## The 7-Step Protocol

1. **BREAK IT DOWN**: Separate request into distinct verifiable claims.
2. **SORT WHAT YOU KNOW**: Classify into Facts, Inferences, Assumptions, Opinions, and Missing/Unknown.
3. **ANSWER WITHOUT GUESSING**: Never invent details, sources, or links to fill gaps.
4. **CHECK YOUR WORK**: Verify logical consistency, bounds, and absence of unsupported bias.
5. **SCORE YOUR CONFIDENCE**: Assign a metric score from 0.0 to 1.0 based on actual evidence.
6. **REVISE IF YOU ARE UNSURE**: If confidence < 0.8, re-evaluate weakest claims or ask clarifying questions.
7. **BE HONEST ABOUT UNCERTAINTY**: Clearly state caveats and missing evidence.
